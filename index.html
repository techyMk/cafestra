Our Story

A quiet ritual,
brewed with intention.

Cafestra began as a small espresso bar with a single roaster and a single belief — that a cup of coffee, made with care, can shift the rhythm of a day.

Today we work directly with estates in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Sumatra, roasting in small batches and serving each cup with the same precision we started with. No shortcuts. No noise. Just craft, calm, and a room that feels a little like home.

The Experience

More than coffee.
A place to land.

01

Atmosphere

Soft lamplight, slow music, well-worn oak. A room designed to slow your breath before you’ve sat down.

02

The Craft

Pour-overs measured to the gram. Espresso pulled to a curve, not a clock. Our baristas train for years — and it shows in the cup.

03

The Beans

Direct-trade, single-origin, micro-lot. Roasted in small batches each Sunday so every bag you take home is just days off the drum.

04

The Room

Fast Wi-Fi, quiet corners, deep tables. Whether you’re writing a novel or finishing a deck — there’s a seat for it here.
